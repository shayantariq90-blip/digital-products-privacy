# Privacy Policy — Digital Products Marketing Assistant

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## 1. Overview

Digital Products Marketing Assistant is a personal tool used by its owner to prepare and manage marketing content for digital products and publish Pins to the owner's Pinterest account.

## 2. Information Accessed

If Pinterest API access is configured, the tool may access the authenticated account's Pinterest profile, boards, and Pins as required for its publishing and duplicate-checking functions.

The tool may also process marketing text, product links, generated images, publishing records, and error logs locally on the owner's computer.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to prepare marketing content, identify available Pinterest boards, publish authorized Pins, prevent duplicate publishing, and record workflow results.

## 4. Storage and Security

Pinterest access credentials are intended to be stored locally and kept out of public source-code repositories. The owner is responsible for protecting the computer, credentials, and local files. No claim is made that all information is encrypted or that unauthorized access is impossible.

## 5. Sharing and Third Parties

Pinterest API requests are sent to Pinterest to perform requested actions. The tool does not intentionally sell personal information. Pinterest's own privacy policy and terms apply to its services.

## 6. Data Retention

Local drafts, generated images, and activity logs may remain on the owner's computer until deleted by the owner.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner through the contact information provided on the associated digital-products website.
